# Code of Conduct

## Standards

Anyone taking part through issues, pull requests or discussions is expected to be respectful and
constructive and to give specific feedback rather than vague criticism.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, abuse or personal attacks of any kind, spam or off-topic promotion and sharing anyone's
private information without consent. That includes details taken from someone's CV.

## Enforcement

> [!NOTE]
> Report unacceptable behaviour to team@vitafolio.isaacadjei.me or via
> [isaacadjei.me/contact](https://isaacadjei.me/contact). Reports are reviewed and appropriate action
> is taken, including removing comments or blocking users where necessary.
